He national flag of Pakistan, officially known as Parcham-e-Sitāra-o-Hilāl (Flag of the Crescent and Star), is a supreme symbol of national pride, unity, and sovereignty. Designed by Syed Amir-uddin Kedwaii and adopted on August 11, 1947, its dark green field represents the Muslim majority, while the vertical white stripe at the hoist symbolizes religious minorities, showcasing the nation's commitment to inclusive rights.
